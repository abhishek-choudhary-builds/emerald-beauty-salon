Our story

A neighbourhood salon that grew on trust

Emerald Beauty Salon is a women-owned salon in the heart of Tollygunge's Charu Market, run by Soni and her team of trained beauty professionals. Today it holds a 4.7 ★ rating from 513 Google reviews — and many guests say they have been coming for five, seven, even ten years or more.

What keeps them loyal is a simple approach: honest advice before every service, professional products, meticulous hygiene, and results that speak for themselves. If a treatment isn't right for your hair or skin, the team will tell you so — and suggest something better.

Questions

Before you visit

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are recommended, and required for some services. Calling ahead means less waiting — especially on weekends.

Is the salon suitable for women who prefer a private environment?

Yes. Emerald is a women-owned salon staffed entirely by women, and guests frequently mention how comfortable and private the environment feels.

How much do services cost?

Pricing depends on hair length, skin type and the treatment chosen, and the team explains everything before starting. Call +91 93397 74868 for today's price list.

Can I bring my daughter?

Yes — the salon is noted as good for kids, and reviews mention children's haircuts and ear piercing done gently.

How do I reach you by public transport?

We're a short walk from Rabindra Sarovar Metro (Gate No. 6), opposite Bhavani Cinema at Charu Market. Tollygunge railway station is about half a kilometre away.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Credit and debit cards, UPI, Google Pay and NFC mobile payments.
